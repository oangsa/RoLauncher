# RoLauncher 1.9.2

Upcoming release.

Launching multiple Roblox accounts now coordinates cleanly without losing client attachment or stalling in Reconnecting states. Managed account close, restart, and recovery proceed normally even alongside external Roblox processes.

Cancelling an unsubmitted launch frees reservations immediately, allowing accounts to restart without a 90-second delay. Discovery polling accelerates to one second while queued accounts wait for client ambiguity to clear.
